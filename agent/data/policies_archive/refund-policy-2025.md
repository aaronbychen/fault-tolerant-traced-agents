title: Refund and return policy
version: 2025.2
effective_date: 2025-03-01
---
Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Items must be unused and in their original packaging. The return window starts
on the delivery date shown on the order. Returns requested more than 30 days
after delivery are not eligible for a refund.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days of
the return being received.
