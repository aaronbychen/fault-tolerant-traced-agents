title: Refund and return policy
version: 2026.1
effective_date: 2026-01-01
---
Customers may return most items within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.
Items must be unused and in their original packaging. The return window starts
on the delivery date shown on the order. Returns requested more than 14 days
after delivery are not eligible for a refund, but may qualify for store credit
under the warranty policy if the item is defective.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of
the return being received.
