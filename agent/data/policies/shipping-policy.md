title: Shipping policy
version: 2026.1
effective_date: 2026-01-01
---
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over $50.
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days for a flat fee of $15.
Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern ship the same business day. Tracking numbers
are emailed when the order ships and appear on the order page.
